How it works

Three steps to an autonomous agent

SPECTRA converts business knowledge into structured specs that an agentic AI can consume directly — without guessing your rules.

STEP 01
Fill the template
Pick your business from the catalogue or fill SPECTRA-PROMPT.md with your domain knowledge — regulations, rules, flows, entities.
STEP 02
LLM generates specs
Send the prompt to Claude, GPT-4o or Gemini. It generates all 13 spec files — glossary, business rules, invariants, workflows, acceptance criteria.
STEP 03
Agent builds
Drop the /specs folder in your repo. The builder agent (Claude Code, Copilot, Cursor) reads the specs and builds the system autonomously.
RESULT
No business questions
The agent knows every rule, invariant and workflow before writing a single line. It builds correctly the first time.
